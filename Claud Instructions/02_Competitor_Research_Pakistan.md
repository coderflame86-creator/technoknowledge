# Technoknowledge: Pakistan Competitor Research (Stage 2)

*Prepared 30 Sep 2026. Desk research from competitors' websites, press coverage and public listings. No product or website redesign in this stage.*

**How to read this**
- **Pricing:** shown only where it is published. Most B2B competitors do not publish school prices.
- **Social media:** assessed from the channels, CTAs and campaigns visible on websites and press. Follower counts and engagement were **not** audited; a platform-by-platform social audit can be a follow-up.
- **Dated sources:** some competitors have thin or dated web presence (RoboGen, RoboMinors, Stemers). This is flagged where it applies.
- **TK baseline:** Technoknowledge facts come from the confirmed baseline document (`01_Current_State_Baseline.md`).

## 1. Market context: why this matters now

The competitive landscape is being reshaped by government action in 2025–26:

| Development | What it means | Source |
|---|---|---|

Schools will look for aligned content, kits and trained teachers | [TechJuice, 19 Jan 2026](https://www.techjuice.pk/pakistan-introduces-ai-robotics-curriculum-for-for-grades-1-12/) · [CW Pakistan](https://cwpakistan.com/pakistan-introduces-ai-and-robotics-curriculum-for-grades-1-to-12/) |

Also, government is doing it itself or with big partners | [ProPakistani, 26 Sep 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/26/punjab-to-train-160000-teachers-under-ai-curriculum/) |
| **Punjab making AI compulsory for Grades 1–5 from 2027–28**; National AI Policy (Jul 2025) targets 1M people trained in AI by 2030 | A private-school "catch-up" market follows public mandates. Parents will expect AI in school | [Lahore Times, 25 Sep 2026](https://www.lhrtimes.com/2026/09/25/ai-education-in-pakistan-our-classrooms-must-catch-up-with-the-future/) |
| National push: 20,000 AI training programmes; AI education authority | More free or subsidised AI training in the market, which puts pressure on paid teacher AI training | [ProPakistani, May 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/05/11/pakistan-to-launch-20000-ai-training-programs/) · [Dig.watch](https://dig.watch/updates/pakistan-approves-ai-education-authority-and-virtual-schools) |

**Implication for this analysis:** being "the school AI & robotics curriculum partner" is quickly becoming a crowded, policy-driven category. Differentiation will come from **alignment with the national curriculum, implementation quality, teacher capability, price-per-student and proof of outcomes**, not from simply "offering AI".

## 5. What the evidence shows (observations only, no redesign)

1. **TK's end-to-end stack is rare among private competitors.** Books + LMS SaaS + AI/robotics curriculum + three ToTs + M&E + government curriculum work: only **Mediatiz** matches the full stack, and Mediatiz is a non-profit with a broader, less tech-focused mission. TechTree, STEMx Global and PSC each cover only part of it.
2. **TK is behind on visible proof and marketing mechanics.**
   - TechTree and STEMx Global use competitions, student project showcases and strong school CTAs.
   - Taleemabad publishes rigorous outcome data.
   - Codingal runs a free-trial funnel.
   - TK has the assets (outcome data, testimonials, projects) but publishes none of them.
3. **Price position is a potential advantage in low-fee schools.** TK's tiered PKR 80–350 per student per month reaches schools that premium players (TechTree) likely don't. Knowledge Platform and Mediatiz are the real rivals in that segment.
4. **"AI for teachers" is getting crowded and cheaper.** Government programmes (Punjab 160k teachers), Taleemabad's free WhatsApp tools and national AI training drives compete with AITEP's PKR 25–30k per teacher. This context helps explain AITEP's 0 paid conversions from 65 Starter attendees.
5. **The national Gr 1–12 AI & Robotics curriculum is the biggest external change.** Every school competitor will claim alignment. TK's SNC curriculum contribution and government track record are credible assets here, but they are not visible on TK's website today.
6. **B2C is crowded and year-round elsewhere.**
   - Codingal, Robokids and Code School PK run continuous, trial-led enrolment. TK runs two seasons a year with about 50 students each.
   - TK's prices are competitive: group PKR 12,999 vs Code School PK Rs 11,159–15,999; 1:1 about PKR 9,200 per 10 hrs vs Robokids Rs 10,000.
   - TK's structural gap is year-round availability and funnel, not price.
7. **Grade coverage gap:** Mediatiz, TechTree, STEMx Global and PSC cover secondary grades (9–12); TK stops at Grade 8.
