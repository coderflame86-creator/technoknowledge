**What this means for the baseline:**
- Technoknowledge is at heart a **B2B SaaS curriculum business** with contracted, recurring monthly revenue. Teacher training and camps are secondary income.
- Retention is structurally protected by the 3-year minimum contracts. **Growth depends on adding new schools**, since the active base (12) is small compared with all-time reach (50+).
- The LMS is the core product: 10 of the 12 schools use it without books.
- AITEP is a new product with an audience (65 Starter attendees) but **no paying customers yet**.

**AITEP in detail (AITEP Proposal, 2026):**
- **Positioning:** "a structured, hands-on approach to integrate Generative AI into everyday academic workflows". Aimed at educators in **schools, colleges and universities**, which is wider than the K–12 focus elsewhere.
- **Scope (8 areas):**
  1. AI-assisted lesson planning
  2. Lecture preparation
  3. Automated PPT development
  4. Assessment and grading automation
  5. Video-based content creation
  6. Voice-based content creation
  7. Interactive learning activities
  8. Textbook-to-teaching-kit conversion (summaries, PPTs, assessments)
- **Learning outcomes:** all of the above, plus **prompt engineering** and **responsible AI / academic integrity**.
- **Expected impact:** less teacher time on planning, grading and content; more consistent, standardized materials; better student engagement; stronger institutional AI readiness; scalable AI practices across departments. These are expected benefits only; no measured results are cited.
- **Price:** the Proposal says PKR 25,000 excl. provincial tax. **Confirmed current price: PKR 25,000–30,000**, **per teacher** (confirmed). **Confirmed duration: 20 hrs.** Batch size is not stated.
- **Tools** (flyer, not the Proposal): ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, Canva AI.

**AITEP Starter (free), as described in the AITEP Proposal:**
- **Format:** 3 hours, online via Zoom; participants need a Gmail account.
- **Audience:** teachers from existing client schools.
- **First run:** Saturday 16 May 2026, 10:00 am–1:00 pm.
- **Sessions:**
  1. Role of teachers in AI literacy, and understanding AI bias (group discussion, bias-spotting activity, fairness case study)
  2. Basic prompt engineering (weak vs improved prompts)
  3. Basic AI image creation and multi-tool AI storytelling
- **Purpose:** "to give a flavor of our new product", i.e. a free taster leading into paid AITEP.

- All programs include **full-year access to digital training resources** and a certificate (website).
- **Robotics ToT flyer:** 40-hour program for Gr 6–8, customized kits, LMS, teacher manuals and lesson plans, STEAM cross-subject alignment, Gr 6→8 progression.
- **Website ToT:** a single program described as both "30-hour (8 days × 4 hrs)" and "40 hours". Covers block and syntax programming, AI, Gen AI, VEX, Android development.
- **ToT clients listed on the website:** The CAS School (2024), Tameer-e-Nau Public School (2023–24), MoFEPT (2024), Sanjan Nagar (2024), ToT Primary (2025).
- **Delivery model:** ToT is sold to schools and government bodies, not to individual teachers. The website states it is "offered to a group of teachers only".
- **Confirmed reach:** 5,000+ teachers trained (all-time).

## 14. Current State of Technoknowledge: baseline summary

It has built a **complete Grades 1–8 computer science curriculum (ITEC)** delivered through **Bit-by-Bit books and a Moodle-based LMS**. The curriculum has AI, Gen AI and robotics (VEX/Arduino) strands, supported by **three teacher-training programs** (ITEC & AI ToT, AITEP, Robotics & IoT ToT), **M&E** and **full-year support**.
