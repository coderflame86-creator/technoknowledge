Common questions

What principals ask us?

Do we need the books to use the LMS?
No. Schools can choose the Bit-by-Bit books, the LMS, or both. The LMS follows the same chapters, so it also works on its own.
Which grades are on the LMS?
Grades 1 to 8, with one course for each grade.
Can students use it at home?
Yes. With a student subscription, students can open lessons, take quizzes and submit assignments from home. Schools can choose a monthly or annual subscription.
Are the video lectures in English?
Yes. All video lectures are in English.
