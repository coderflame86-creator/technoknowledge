What your students will learn

From first click to their own apps

A step-by-step path from Grade 1 to Grade 8, starting with block-based coding and growing into real programming languages.

  1. 01

    Computer fundamentals

    How computers work, digital skills and safe, confident use of technology.

  2. 02

    Logic and problem solving

    Breaking down problems, designing algorithms and drawing flowcharts.

  3. 03

    Coding and programming

    From Scratch Jr and Scratch to Code.org, HTML and Python.

  4. 04

    Application development

    Designing and building working mobile apps with MIT App Inventor.

Common questions

What principals ask us?

Can ITEC replace our current computer textbooks?
Yes. ITEC is a complete, full-year computer curriculum for Grades 1–8. Schools can use the books, the LMS, or both.
Do our teachers need coding experience?
No. Training starts from the fundamentals and builds up to Scratch projects, app development and Python.
What does the school need for training?
A computer for each teacher and a stable internet connection. The in-person sessions take place at your campus.
Can students get their own access to the curriculum?
Yes. Schools can take a monthly or annual student subscription to the digitized ITEC curriculum on our LMS, with interactive assessments, assignments, real-time results and student profiling. Request a quote for your number of students.
What language is the content in?
All curriculum content and video lectures are in English.
Do you provide the ITEC coding curriculum to schools across Pakistan?
Yes. We work with schools and education departments in the Federal capital, Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Gilgit-Baltistan. Teacher training combines in-person and online sessions, and the LMS works anywhere with an internet connection. Read our guide to Pakistan's AI and robotics curriculum.
