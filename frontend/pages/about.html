Our story

Built for Pakistani classrooms since 2021.

We started in Karachi in January 2021 with a simple belief: children in Pakistan should not only use technology, they should be able to create it.

That belief became ITEC, a full-year computer science curriculum for Grades 1 to 8, taught through our Bit-by-Bit books and our LMS. Around it we built what schools told us they needed: teacher training, robotics and IoT, AI for teachers through AITEP, and monitoring and evaluation to keep quality high all year.

Today we work with private schools, school networks, NGOs and government education departments, from Karachi to Gilgit-Baltistan.

  1. 2021

    Founded in Karachi

    Technoknowledge (SMC-Pvt) Ltd. begins with a creative-technology computer science curriculum for schools.

  2. A national curriculum contribution

    We contributed to the Single National Curriculum for Computer Science, Grades 6 to 8.

  3. Government programs

    Teacher and student training with federal and provincial education departments in four regions.

  4. Recognised

    Best Women-led Startup at NIC Islamabad, and a STEM.org accredited curriculum.

  5. Today

    50,000+ students, 5,000+ teachers

    Curriculum, LMS, teacher training, robotics and AI programs for schools, plus Techno Camps for students.
