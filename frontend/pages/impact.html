Partners and projects

Projects with education departments.

We have trained teachers, rolled out our curriculum and helped shape the national Computer Science curriculum with federal and provincial education bodies.

  • 2021

    Federal Directorate of EducationIslamabad

    Coding for the early years

    45teachers trained

    We trained 45 teachers from ICB and ICG schools under the FDE in coding and developing technologies, to bring children in their early years to the creator side of technology. Teachers learned to deliver ITEC through our Learning Management System.

  • Summer Fiesta

    FDE Summer FiestaFederal Ministry of Education

    AI in the classroom

    40teachers trained

    At the Federal Ministry of Education’s Summer Fiesta, we trained 40 teachers in AI and Generative AI, with a focus on using these tools in class to improve learning.

  • Tech Fellows

    Ministry of Education, Gilgit-BaltistanGilgit-Baltistan

    Training 150 Tech Fellows

    150Tech Fellows trained

    We partnered with the Ministry of Education to train 150 Tech Fellows to teach coding and developing technologies, so their students become producers of technology, not just users.

  • ITEC roll-out

    Sindh Education FoundationSindh

    ITEC in 25 SEF schools

    25schools · 45 teachers trained

    We built the skills of 45 teachers in coding, programming, developing technologies and teaching methods, and rolled out ITEC in 25 SEF schools to strengthen digital literacy.

  • 2024

    Ministry of Federal Education & Professional TrainingIslamabad

    Training the trainers

    ToTTrain-the-Trainer program

    A Train-the-Trainer program for MoFEPT teachers: we train lead teachers, who then train others in their schools.

  • Curriculum

    Single National CurriculumNational

    Shaping the national curriculum

    Gr 6–8Computer Science curriculum

    We helped develop the Computer Science curriculum for Grades 6 to 8 in the Single National Curriculum, bringing in coding, algorithmic thinking and computational thinking.
