Common questions

What principals ask us?

How quickly will you reply?
Within one working day, on WhatsApp, by phone or by email.
Can we combine programs?
Yes. Choose as many as you like in the form, and we will send one proposal that brings them together.
Can we start small?
Yes. Many schools begin with a free 3-hour AITEP Starter session for their teachers, or with a few grades, then expand.
Do you work outside Karachi?
Yes. We have worked with schools and education departments across Federal, Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Gilgit-Baltistan.
Is training on campus or online?
Both. Training can be on campus, online or a mix of the two, planned around your timetable.
