What Do Children Build in a Techno Camp?

What happens in a Techno Camp: live online courses for ages 6 to 12+, from Scratch Jr. and Canva to Python, app development and Generative AI.

4 October 20262 min readBy the Technoknowledge team

{}

On this page

  1. Ages 6 to 8: think, create and design
  2. Ages 9 to 12: code games, apps and AI
  3. Ages 12 and up: real programming and AI
  4. How do the camps work?
  5. How to join?

Share

Parents often ask us what their child will actually do in a Techno Camp. The short answer: they build things. Every course is live and online with a real teacher, and every course ends in a project your child made.

Here is what that looks like at each age.

Ages 6 to 8: think, create and design

Younger children start with the thinking behind coding, then make their first creations:

  • Algorithm Designing: break a problem into small steps and put them in order
  • Logic Building: puzzles and games that build “if this, then that” thinking
  • Scratch Jr: drag-and-drop coding for animated stories and simple games
  • Canva: design cards, posters and stories

Ages 9 to 12: code games, apps and AI

At this age children are ready for real projects:

  • Computational Thinking: solve problems like a programmer
  • Scratch: code games, animations and interactive stories
  • Canva: posters, presentations and social posts
  • Python: first real code, with drawings, quizzes and mini-games
  • Java: the building blocks of a language used in apps and games
  • App Development: working mobile apps with MIT App Inventor
  • Generative AI: create with AI tools, safely and responsibly

Ages 12 and up: real programming and AI

Older students go further with real languages and tools:

  • Python: from basics to small programs and games
  • Java: object-based programming used in real software
  • Web Development: design and build a website with HTML and CSS
  • Graphics Designing: logos, posters and social media designs
  • App Development: complete mobile apps with MIT App Inventor
  • Generative AI: write, design and build with AI
  • Agentic AI: simple AI agents that plan and complete tasks

Scratch, Python and Generative AI are our most popular courses.

How do the camps work?

  • Live online: a real teacher, in real time, from home
  • 24 hours per course
  • Group classes in small groups of children the same age, during the summer and winter breaks: PKR 12,999 per course
  • 1-to-1 classes all year, with flexible days and timings: PKR 21,999 per course
  • No experience needed: every course starts from the basics
  • What your child needs: a laptop, a camera and mic, and a good internet connection

Course outlines are shared on request.

How to join?

Choose a course by your child’s age on our Techno Camps page and fill in the short registration form. We confirm the class type, timing and fee with you on WhatsApp.
