Robotics & IoT teacher training

No electronics background needed.

Teachers start from the basics and build the same projects their students will, over about 40 hours in 8 days.

  1. Day 1Orientation, pre-assessment, tools and hardware
  2. Days 2–7Hands-on projects: soldering, sensors, Arduino programming, motors and IoT
  3. Day 8Post-assessment, feedback and certificates

Every teacher gets

  • LMS login with all training material for the contract period
  • Training pack with exercises and assessments
  • Pre- and post-training assessment
  • Certificate of participation

Tools covered

  • Arduino IDE
  • Tinkercad
  • PictoBlox
  • Scratch
  • PhET simulations
  • RodoCodo

Sensors: ultrasonic, IR, gas (MQ-2), soil moisture and servo motors.

The school provides: a computer lab, Wi-Fi, a projector, power backup, flat work tables and a laptop for each teacher.

Common questions

What principals ask us?

Do our teachers need an engineering background?
No. Training starts with the basics of machines, robots and programming, and builds up project by project.
Are robotics kits included?
Kits are purchased separately by the school. We advise on the right kits for your grades and class sizes.
What does the school need for teacher training?
A computer lab, Wi-Fi, a projector, power sockets with electricity backup, flat work tables, and a laptop for each teacher.
Which grades does the program cover?
Grades 1–5 follow a VEX Robotics-based curriculum. Grades 6–8 build hands-on Arduino and IoT projects.
Do you offer the robotics and IoT curriculum to schools across Pakistan?
Yes. We work with schools in the Federal capital, Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Gilgit-Baltistan. Teacher training is hands-on, and we advise each school on the right kits for its grades. Read our guide to Pakistan's AI and robotics curriculum.
