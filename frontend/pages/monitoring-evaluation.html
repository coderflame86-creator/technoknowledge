Common questions

What principals ask us?

Who do you interview?
Teachers, students and school administration, so the report reflects every side of the classroom.
Will visits disrupt classes?
No. Visits and assessments are scheduled with the school in advance and fit around your timetable.
What do we get at the end of each cycle?
A grade-wise progress report that highlights issues and recommends specific changes.
Which plan is right for us?
Quarterly suits schools that want close tracking, bi-annual suits most schools, and annual gives a full-year overview. We can help you decide.
Is M&E included with ITEC?
Yes. Monitoring and evaluation is part of the ITEC program. Schools that do not use ITEC can book M&E on its own.
