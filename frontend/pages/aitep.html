Common questions

What principals ask us?

Do teachers need technical skills?
No. If a teacher can use email and a web browser, they can join AITEP.
What do teachers need?
A laptop or computer, an internet connection and a Gmail account. Most tools we use have free versions.
Can we start with some teachers only?
Yes. Many schools start with one department or section, then extend to all staff.
How is pricing worked out?
The full program is PKR 25,000–30,000 per teacher, excluding provincial tax. Request a proposal and we will share an exact quote for your school.
Is AITEP available for schools across Pakistan?
Yes. AITEP runs on campus, online or hybrid, so schools in any city in Pakistan can join. Start with a free 3-hour online Starter session.
