Common questions

What principals ask us?

Can we order the books only?
Yes. Schools can choose the Bit-by-Bit books, the LMS, or both. Request a proposal and we will share a quote for your grades and number of students.
What do teachers get with the books?
Notes for teachers, a teachers' guide in every chapter, and a curriculum pack on our LMS with lectures, lesson plans, a schema of work, interactive videos and extra assessments.
Do you train our teachers?
Yes. ITEC teacher training is 40 hours: 24 hours in person and 16 hours online, with a certificate.
Is there a digital version for students?
Yes. Schools can add a monthly or annual student LMS subscription with the digitized curriculum, interactive assessments, assignments and real-time results.
How do we see the full books?
The previews show selected sample pages. Request a proposal or message us on WhatsApp and we will walk you through the full books for your grades.
