Questions parents ask

Before you register

My child has never coded. Can they join?
Yes. No prior experience is needed. Every course starts from the basics.
When are group classes available?
Group classes run only during term breaks (summer and winter). 1-to-1 classes are available all year.
Are classes online?
Yes. All classes are live and online, so your child learns from home with a real teacher.
Can I see the course outline first?
Yes. Outlines are shared on request. Message us on WhatsApp.
Can my child take more than one course?
Yes. Add all the courses you're interested in and we'll suggest the best order.
Are Techno Camps coding bootcamps?
Yes. Techno Camps are short, project-based coding and AI bootcamps for kids aged 6 to 12+. Each course ends with a project your child has made.
Can children outside Pakistan join?
Yes. All bootcamps are live and online, so children from any city in Pakistan or anywhere in the world can join from home. We have trained kids across the globe.
How much does a bootcamp cost?
A group class is PKR 12,999 per course and runs in the summer and winter breaks. A 1-to-1 class is PKR 21,999 per course and runs all year round.
