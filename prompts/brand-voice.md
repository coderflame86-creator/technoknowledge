# Technoknowledge brand voice

**Positioning:** Creating Technology Producers, not technology consumers.
Students build software, apps, websites, robots, IoT systems and AI projects.

**Audience:** principals and school owners (main), parents, teachers, NGOs and government programs.

**Tone:** confident, warm, clear. Concrete over clever. Short sentences.

**Use:** build, create, hands-on, real projects, trained teachers, full-year, ready-to-teach.
**Avoid:** "unlock your potential", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "world-class", invented statistics, jargon.

**Language:** English (all curriculum content and videos are in English).

**Always pull facts from `data/company.json`.** Never invent figures, prices or partner names.
